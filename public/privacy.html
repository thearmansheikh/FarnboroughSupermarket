What we collect

If you send an enquiry, we collect your name, email address and message. We also store your cookie-category choices in your browser so we can remember them.

Why we use your information

We use enquiry details to respond to your questions about our shop, products, opening hours or directions. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests in responding to customer enquiries. Optional analytics, marketing and automatic map loading are off unless you choose them. The map can also be loaded following your explicit click on “Load map”.

Third-party services

  • FormSubmit processes and delivers enquiry forms. It retains submissions in its archive for up to 30 days. Read the FormSubmit privacy policy.
  • Google Maps is contacted only after you allow the Maps category or select “Load map”. Google may then set its own cookies or storage. Read Google's privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts this website and processes technical request information needed to deliver it. Read Vercel's privacy policy.

How long we keep information

We propose deleting enquiry emails after 12 months. FormSubmit separately retains submitted records in its archive for up to 30 days. Cookie preferences remain in local browser storage until you change them or clear that storage.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask to access, rectify or erase your personal data, restrict or object to processing, and request data portability. Contact us using the details above to exercise these rights.

Complaints

You have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office or call the ICO on 0303 123 1113.
