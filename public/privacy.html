Privacy Policy

We collect personal information only when you contact us through this website or by phone. This may include your name, email address and the message you send.

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry about our store, products, opening hours or directions.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information is kept only for as long as needed to respond to the enquiry and to maintain accurate contact records.

If you want to update or remove your information, please contact us directly by phone or email and we will do our best to help.

For any questions about your personal information, contact us at supermarketfarnborough@gmail.com or call 01252 940815.
