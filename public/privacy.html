Privacy Policy

This website uses essential browser storage for site preferences and to remember your cookie choices. You can accept all, reject optional categories, or manage analytics and marketing preferences. The website does not currently load analytics or advertising tools, so those choices are saved as consent preferences but do not enable tracking tools.

When you submit an enquiry, we collect your name, email address and message so the shop can respond to you.

Enquiry forms are delivered by FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service. FormSubmit retains submission records in its archive for up to 30 days. See the FormSubmit privacy policy for details about its handling of submitted information.

We use your information to respond to your enquiry about our store, products, opening hours or directions. We do not sell or use your personal data for marketing.

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond and maintain accurate contact records. You can contact us to request an update or removal.

If you want to update or remove your information, please contact us directly by phone or email and we will do our best to help.

For any questions about your personal information, contact us at supermarketfarnborough@gmail.com or call 01252 940815.
