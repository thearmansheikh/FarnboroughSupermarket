# Project Roadmap

The detailed plan, with findings and tasks, is in [site-plan.md](site-plan.md). Owner tasks are in [launch-todos.md](launch-todos.md).

## Phase 0: Land what exists
- Commit the launch-preparation work
- Remove the old checkout and success pages
- Bring the docs in line with the showcase-only site

## Phase 1: Honest content
- Replace stock photos with real store photos
- Confirm or remove unverified claims

## Phase 2: Engineering cleanup
- Build Tailwind ahead of time and remove the CDN script
- Share one header and footer across pages
- Add security headers and more tests

## Phase 3: Local SEO
- Structured data, titles and descriptions, sitemap dates, Google Business Profile

## Phase 4: Accessibility and QA
- Skip link, focus states, Lighthouse and axe checks, phone testing

## Phase 5: Go live
- Custom domain, environment variables, FormSubmit activation, final privacy wording

## Phase 6: After launch
- Monitor enquiries and 404s, refresh photos and offers, decide on analytics
