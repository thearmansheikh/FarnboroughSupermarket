## 7. Asking for reviews

Share the profile's review link (Profile → "Ask for reviews") at the till, on the Facebook page and in a QR code by the door. Suggested message:

> Thanks for shopping with us at Farnborough Supermarket. If you were happy with your visit, a quick Google review really helps other local families find us.

Rules: never pay for or write reviews yourself, and never ask only happy customers selectively in a way that breaks Google's policy. Reply to every review, good or bad, politely and briefly.

## 8. Keep details identical everywhere

The name, address and phone number must match exactly on Google, Facebook, the website footer and any directory. The website's structured data and footer are in `src/partials/`.

## 9. Search Console (after the custom domain is live)

1. Go to search.google.com/search-console and add a **Domain** property for the live domain.
2. Verify with the DNS record Google shows, added at the domain registrar.
3. Open Sitemaps and submit `sitemap.xml`.
4. Use URL Inspection on the home page and the Products and Contact pages and choose "Request indexing".
5. After about two weeks, check Performance → Queries, and Pages → "Why pages aren't indexed" for problems.
