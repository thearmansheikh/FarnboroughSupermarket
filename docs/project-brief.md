# Project Brief

## 1. Project name

- Farnborough Supermarket

## 2. Problem being solved

Farnborough residents need a clear, modern and trustworthy place to discover a local supermarket that offers fresh produce, halal meat, pantry staples and world food essentials without the friction of a complicated online checkout experience.

## 3. Target users

- Local families shopping for weekly essentials
- Muslim households looking for fresh halal meat and pantry staples
- Multicultural shoppers seeking rice, spices, tea and international products
- Busy commuters and residents wanting a nearby convenient store with clear opening hours

## 4. Main goals

- Present the supermarket as a reliable local option for everyday shopping
- Highlight fresh food, product variety and family-friendly convenience
- Increase awareness of location, opening hours and direct contact details
- Create a polished storefront that can be shared with customers and prospects

## 5. Core features

- Strong homepage with clear value proposition and store highlights
- Product categories and showcase pages for fresh produce, meat, rice and world foods
- Store information section with address, contact number and business hours
- Gallery and about page to build trust and local identity
- Mobile-friendly design for browsing on the go

## 6. Non-goals

- Online ordering and payment processing
- Inventory tracking or customer account management
- Multi-location support
- Full ecommerce checkout workflow beyond the demo experience

## 7. Success metrics

- Visitors can quickly understand what the business sells and why it matters
- Store phone number and directions are easy to find
- Page engagement improves across homepage, products and contact journey
- The site feels polished and trustworthy enough to support local brand awareness

## 8. Notes

The business is positioned as a neighbourhood supermarket with strong fresh food and family essentials focus. The current experience is best considered a marketing and storefront website rather than a full ecommerce platform.
